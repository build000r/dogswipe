## 1. What DogSwipe Is

DogSwipe is a community platform where neighbors post small local offerings — food, baked goods, produce, and other homemade items — and other neighbors discover and claim them using community credits. It is a local barter economy, not a traditional marketplace.

## 2. Community Credits

### 2.1 Purchasing Credits

Credits are purchased 1:1 with U.S. dollars (e.g., $10 buys 10 credits) through the in-app payment flow. This is the only way real money enters the DogSwipe system.

### 2.2 Non-Withdrawable — No Cash-Out, Ever

**Credits cannot be withdrawn, refunded to cash, or converted back to dollars — not by buyers, not by makers, not by anyone.** This is not a limitation or a missing feature; it is a core design choice. When you buy credits, those dollars enter the community credit pool permanently. There is no cash-out button and there never will be.

### 2.3 How Credits Are Spent

Credits can only be spent on other people's offerings within DogSwipe. You cannot:

- Withdraw credits to a bank account, payment card, or any fiat currency
- Transfer credits to another user outside of claiming an offering
- Self-deal (spend credits on your own offerings)
- Redeem credits for cash or cash equivalents from the platform or any third party

### 2.4 Credits Earned by Makers

When someone claims your offering, you earn credits. Those earned credits work exactly like purchased credits — spendable only on other neighbors' offerings within DogSwipe, never withdrawable to cash. Earned credits may constitute taxable barter income; see Section 8.

### 2.5 Credit Balance and Ledger

Your credit balance is derived from an append-only ledger of all purchases, earnings, and spends. Balances are never hand-edited. Every credit movement is recorded and auditable.

## 3. The Community Credit Pool (Float)

The dollars behind outstanding credits are held by the platform as a **community liability** — money owed to the credit pool, not platform revenue. The platform does not treat credit-purchase dollars as income or profit. This pool exists solely to back the credits in circulation.

## 4. Disputes and Refunds

### 4.1 Credit-Only Refunds

If a claim is disputed (e.g., an offering was not delivered or was materially different from its description), the platform may issue a **credit refund**. Disputed claims are resolved by returning credits to the claimer's balance through an audited, append-only ledger entry.

### 4.2 No Cash Refunds

**Disputes are never resolved by returning dollars.** The no-cash-out rule applies to refunds without exception. When you purchase credits, the dollar transaction is final. Dispute resolution operates entirely within the credit system.

### 4.3 Dispute Process

Either party to a claim (maker or claimer) may flag a dispute after the claim is created. The platform will review disputed claims and may:

- Refund credits to the claimer
- Adjust credits through an audited ledger correction
- Take no action if the dispute is unfounded

All dispute resolutions are recorded as append-only ledger entries and are visible in your transaction history.

## 5. Food and Offering Safety

### 5.1 Platform Role

DogSwipe is a discovery and matching platform. **DogSwipe does not prepare, inspect, handle, store, or deliver any food or other offerings.** All offerings are created, prepared, and handed off directly between individual community members.

### 5.2 Maker Responsibilities

If you post an offering on DogSwipe, you represent that:

- You are solely responsible for the safety, quality, and legality of your offering
- You will comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws, including food safety and cottage food regulations where applicable
- You will accurately describe your offering, including relevant allergen information
- You understand that food items requiring temperature control (including but not limited to meat, dairy, and eggs) may be subject to stricter regulations than shelf-stable items in your jurisdiction
- You will label home-prepared food items as required by your local cottage food laws, where applicable

### 5.3 Claimer Acknowledgment

By claiming an offering on DogSwipe, you acknowledge that:

- The offering is prepared by an individual community member, not a licensed commercial establishment (unless the maker has independently obtained such licensing)
- DogSwipe has not inspected, tested, or verified the safety or quality of the offering
- You accept the offering at your own risk
- You are responsible for communicating any dietary restrictions or allergies to the maker before hand-off

### 5.4 Hand-Off

Offerings are exchanged directly between makers and claimers. A claim is not considered complete until both parties confirm the hand-off. DogSwipe is not a party to the physical exchange.

## 6. Acceptable Use

You agree not to:

- Use DogSwipe to conduct transactions outside the credit system (e.g., arranging cash payments through the platform)
- Attempt to circumvent the no-cash-out design by any means, including informal credit trading or off-platform value exchange
- Post offerings you cannot or do not intend to fulfill
- Misrepresent the nature, quality, or contents of an offering
- Create multiple accounts to manipulate credits, reviews, or discovery ranking
- Use the platform for any illegal purpose

## 7. Reviews and Trust

After a confirmed hand-off, both the maker and claimer may review each other. Reviews are gated to completed claims — you cannot review someone you have not transacted with. Aggregate reputation from reviews may surface in discovery ranking. Fraudulent or abusive reviews may be removed.

## 8. Tax Considerations

Credits earned by fulfilling offerings may constitute taxable barter income under U.S. tax law. The IRS generally taxes barter transactions at fair market value. Because credits are purchased at par (1:1 with dollars), the fair market value of earned credits is straightforward to calculate.

**DogSwipe does not provide tax advice.** You are responsible for reporting any taxable income from credit earnings to the appropriate tax authorities. The platform may be required to issue tax reporting forms (e.g., 1099) if your credit earnings exceed applicable thresholds.

## 9. Account Termination

- Your remaining credit balance is forfeited — credits cannot be refunded to cash under any circumstance
- Your ledger history is retained for audit and compliance purposes
- Active claims in progress will be resolved before termination where possible

The platform does not collect or emit personally identifiable information in analytics. A full Privacy Policy is published separately.
